Machine Learning Underwriting Platform

Welcome to CREDIVEX. This system uses a Support Vector Classifier (SVC) with an RBF kernel to evaluate loan applications, identify credit risk, and provide transparent probability scores.

Automated credit decisions powered by Support Vector AI

Our model analyzes 6 core financial indicators ג€” income, loan size, credit score, debt-to-income ratio, employment tenure, and delinquency history ג€” to produce reliable, data-driven loan decisions.

Model Accuracy (ROC-AUC) 91.46% Validated against test portfolio

ROC AUC (Receiver Operating Characteristic Area Under Curve) evaluates the model's ability to distinguish between approved and rejected applicants. A score of 0.9146 means the model ranks a random approved applicant higher than a random rejected applicant 91.46% of the time, ensuring highly reliable approval/rejection reasoning.

Algorithm SVC (RBF Kernel)
Regularization (C) 7.308
Kernel Gamma (־³) 0.200
Support Vectors 14,794

Non-Linear Decision Margin

Uses the RBF kernel to capture complex relationships between debt ratios and income that standard linear scoring misses.

Calibrated Probabilities

Transforms raw hyperplane distance into smooth 0% to 100% confidence probabilities for clear, interpretable risk grading.

Standardized Pipeline

Pre-processed with StandardScaler Z-score normalization to ensure balanced weight across all numeric feature distributions.

How to interpret evaluation results

01

Probability Dial

Confidence score: $\ge 50\%$ indicates loan approval, with $\ge 80\%$ representing prime, low-risk borrowers.

02

Decision Margin

Distance from the separating hyperplane. Positive values indicate a safe margin; negative values denote elevated risk.

03

Benchmark Comparison

    Pipeline Architecture

    1
    StandardScaler (Z-Score Normalization)

    Standardizes all 6 input dimensions with zero mean and unit variance so no single feature dominates the distance calculation.

    2
    SVC (RBF Kernel)

    Evaluates standardized features against 14,794 support vectors using $C=7.308$ and $\gamma=0.2$.

    3
    Platt Scaling Calibration

    Converts raw margin distances $f(x)$ into calibrated probabilities between 0% and 100%.
